# [REGRESSION] Audit criteria substance lost in the #2490 re-rip

## 1. Intent and Executive Summary

| # | Field | Description |

| 1 | **Problem Statement** | The deck's audit surface regressed into a hollow form in the #2490 re-rip: the audit vocabulary survived — the floor's `audit` / `remediate [and re-audit]` utterances, the verify card's fixed-criteria-set requirement — but the criteria that vocabulary points at did not. Observed 2026-10-13 by auditing the pre-rip deck (preserved under `attic/`, tag `pre-rip`) against the live deck. Six facets: (1) plans are never audited — no plan-side criteria set exists anywhere in the deck and the plan card has no validation dispatch, so a plan reaches `implement` unchecked; (2) implementation audits lost the test-quality families (edge-case completeness, cross-boundary coverage, assertion-weakening detection, RED evidence, sequential RED→GREEN per item) and the reverse drift direction (implementation beyond the spec, spec-required surfaces missing, signature mismatch); (3) the spec validation criteria set (7 defect classes, `spec/references/validation-standards.md`) covers only the SC-table layer of what the pre-rip spec-validation pipeline checked — the pre-rip spec-audit's analysis families (reasoning soundness, claim accuracy, blast radius, research adequacy, gap analysis, scope narrowness, cross-reference sufficiency), the holistic dimensions the current classes do not cover (escape hatches, feasibility, safety), and the six decomposition criteria that lived in the producer-side validate step (#2116's target, `attic/skills/spec-creation/tasks/validate.md`; the #2117 attempt to add them to the audit evaluator closed without implementation) are all absent; (4) the evidence-type rule survives only as two named examples instead of the instrument-capability principle — an instrument that cannot in principle detect the criterion's failure mode produces no evidence; (5) `routing.md` has no row matching an on-demand audit of an existing artifact, though the floor vocabulary names `audit` and `remediate [and re-audit]`; (6) the writer side has no audit-readiness discipline — the spec card's creation steps govern what goes into the artifact but never direct the writer to check the finished draft against the bar it will be judged by, and the validation standards reference is positioned as the reviewer's criteria only, so mechanical-consistency defects surface solely through the fresh-context dispatch. Observed 2026-10-13 on this issue's own analysis→spec flow: three FAIL rounds before the clean PASS, and all eight findings across those rounds were writer-catchable mechanical-consistency defects — coverage asymmetry between the named-loss inventory and the restoration inventory (three instances, one exposed by a count error), a provenance claim asserted without verifying its source, count and method-range drift after edits, a self-contradictory cost frame, and a false current-state claim about the live deck. The developer directed the same day that the writer cards aim at audit passage from first creation — reducing re-audit/revise loop work, explicitly not a 100% hit target. |
| 2 | **Root Cause / Motivation** | The #2490 rip-and-replace retired the pre-rip `audit` card wholesale — roughly 17,000 lines across twelve audit types, each a 4-role Investigator → Validator → Evaluator → Arbiter chain (sources under `attic/skills/audit/tasks/`) — together with the plan-fidelity criteria (`attic/skills/audit/tasks/plan-fidelity-evaluator.md`), the test-quality criteria (`attic/skills/audit/tasks/test-quality-audit-evaluator.md`), the drift-detection criteria (`attic/skills/audit/tasks/drift-detection-evaluator.md`), the holistic dimension definitions (`attic/reference/holistic-dimensions.yaml`), and the decomposition criteria (`attic/audit-reference/reference/decomposition-criteria.md`). Subsequent restorations rebuilt pieces around the gap — #2550 the single-reviewer verdict semantics, #2557 the release gate, #2597 the artifact-presence gate — but no restoration rebuilt the criteria sets themselves, leaving the deck's audit dispatch language pointing at a criteria base that no longer exists. The producer side lost its aim with the same retirement: the pre-rip deck carried an explicit producer self-check concept (`producer_self_checks` in `attic/reference/holistic-dimensions.yaml`), but as separate producer checklist tasks feeding second copies of the criteria, and it retired with the machinery — the #2550 restoration rebuilt only the reviewer side. |
| 3 | **Approach Chosen** | Restore criteria depth, not audit machinery, per the developer's 2026-10-13 direction (determine the card set; customize text per card; least ceremony, no floor growth). Seven surfaces: (1) `spec` — two surfaces in one card. The card's validation step (step 6) gains the producer-side self-check: before dispatching the fresh-context reviewer, the writer applies the same validation standards to the completed draft in their own context and remediates what they find — a duty-of-care pass whose purpose is to spend the fresh-context dispatch on judgment-level defects rather than mechanical-consistency ones; the self-check never replaces the dispatch and a self-passed verdict is no verdict. And its criteria reference (`spec/references/validation-standards.md`) — defect classes expanded to carry the restored analysis families, each judgment-phrased for reviewer judgment with no keyword lists: bidirectional traceability (orphan SCs and untested root causes are both findings), fabricated claims (asserted without source evidence; negation claims without exhaustive verification behind them), internal contradiction (explicit, implicit, scope), escape hatches (language licensing the implementer to short-circuit the requirements), feasibility (references to non-existent files, functions, libraries, or capabilities), gap analysis (implicit preconditions, untested boundary conditions), scope narrowness (symptom-only fix, over-scoping, systemic implication left unaddressed), research adequacy (alternatives not ruled out; code-state claims without recency verification), blast radius (the change's impact enumeration is incomplete — affected consumers, dependents, or non-code surfaces such as cross-references, tests, or docs the change touches are unaccounted for), cross-reference sufficiency (a claim citing a source the source does not support, or a load-bearing claim left uncited), decomposition (atomicity, single deliverable, binary verifiability, PR-gate viability, ceremony, covered-by-prior), and defect class 5 sharpened to the general instrument-capability form (existence cannot verify content, pattern-match cannot verify meaning, reading cannot verify runtime effect — the instrument must be able to detect the criterion's failure), plus safety (failure modes that could cause irreversible harm — destructive operations without rollback paths, data-loss scenarios, irreversible operations without safeguards). (2) `plan` — a new plan-side criteria reference (`plan/references/plan-validation-standards.md`) re-authoring the plan-side families for the per-SC item format (item coverage in both directions, explicit acyclic ordering, instrument reality, scope discipline, safety, plus the shared families), self-contained with its own verdict contract and revise→re-validate loop; the plan card gains a fresh-context validation dispatch step executed before `implement` consumes the plan on the full path, carrying the same pre-dispatch self-check against the same reference, phrased in the plan card's own voice. (3) `implement` — the test-quality families integrated with the existing test-value gate (edge cases and error conditions relevant to the item's behavior, cross-boundary assertion reality where the change crosses module boundaries, no assertion weakening without the asserted behavior changing, each RED confirmed failing before its GREEN, sequential per-item cycles rather than batch RED then batch GREEN), and the SC-fidelity rule extended with the reverse drift direction in all three modes (no deliverable the spec or plan does not state; no spec-required surface left missing; every interface contract the spec or plan states is matched as written — a present-but-divergent surface is a finding), with drift in any direction reported, never silently absorbed. (4) `verify` — the instrument-capability rule stated as a general reviewer rule with the behavioral-provenance step as an instance of it, and the criteria-set ownership mechanism: the audited artifact's producing card owns that artifact's audit criteria reference and the audit reviewer reads it — composing with, not replacing, the existing criteria-invariance rule. (5) `routing.md` — one row routing an on-demand audit of an existing artifact to the verify card with the artifact's criteria set. (6) Ledger entry in #2534 (attic sources, #2490 retirement, observed failure, placements, declined candidates). |

| 5 | **Key Design Decisions** | (1) The behavioral/structural classification stays binary — the design question the developer ratified on 2026-10-13 (least ceremony, no fat always-injected surface); the instrument-capability principle replaces the taxonomy, and `floor.md` gains nothing. (6) Re-wording is in scope: each affected card's existing text may be re-worded to carry the restored rules in that card's own voice; the SCs bind the semantic elements each card must state, not literal wording; re-wording must not alter the cards' other semantics. (7) The attic is source material only — restoration is re-authoring per the developer's customization directive; the ledger entry names the source paths so future audits can verify the derivation. ### Cost Frame

Criteria-depth restoration across five surfaces (spec card + reference, plan card + reference, implement, verify, routing), one ledger entry — the smallest surface that gives the deck's audit dispatch language its criteria base back and points the writer at it from first creation. Growth lands in load-on-demand references (the `validation-standards.md` expansion and the new `plan-validation-standards.md`) plus a few lines in four card bodies (spec's existing validation step, plan's new dispatch step, implement, verify); `routing.md` grows by one row; `floor.md` grows by nothing (SC-9). No behavioral SCs: the criteria are intent-decidable instruments applied by reviewer judgment, and scripting agent behavior against them would re-create the deck's founding defect. The dispatch shape is proven — the spec card's step-6 validation dispatch and the verify reviewer already run; the one new dispatch this change adds is the plan validation dispatch (SC-3), reusing that shape rather than inventing a second verdict protocol. The producer-side self-checks (SC-3, SC-13) add zero dispatch cost — they are the writer's own-context pass over the same reference — and are expected to reduce the revise→re-validate loop's entry cost on the observed evidence: all eight findings in this issue's own three-FAIL-round validation were writer-catchable mechanical-consistency defects. The structural SCs make the restored text falsifiable; every future audit applies the criteria by judgment.
