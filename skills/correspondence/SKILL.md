# correspondence — stakeholder communication

1. **Audience separation.** Internal findings, audit output, and raw progress
   go to chat — never to stakeholder channels. Stakeholder content is drafted
   deliberately or not at all.
2. **Executive summary format.** Lead with the outcome; details follow in
   descending importance; active voice; no filler.
3. **Every URL verified** before it appears in correspondence — a dead or
   fabricated link in stakeholder content is a credibility defect.
4. **Honest status.** Blockers and risks are stated plainly; a rosy-but-wrong
   status update is worse than an uncomfortable truth.
5. **Identity byline** on sent correspondence per attribution rules.
